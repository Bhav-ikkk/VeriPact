# VeriPact Demo Video — Voiceover Script (timed to the recorded v5 video)

Total: ~350 spoken words ≈ 140 seconds at 150 wpm (limit 3:00).
Beats follow `npm run demo:record` (8 beats, wallet arc first).
No cryptographic acronyms are spoken. Numbers are written as digits
for the narrator's timing sheet; speak them naturally.

## 0:00–0:20 | Hook: Agents Spend, VeriPact Clears Intent

Shopping agents are about to spend your money. Buyer controls can
limit how much — but nothing proves what the merchant actually
promised. VeriPact
fixes that: it pairs your agent's wallet, caps every payment, and
locks the merchant's promise inside the PayPal order itself.

## 0:20–0:45 | Beats 1–2: QR Pairing, $350 Partition Wall, Refund Undo

Watch the wallet. A QR code pairs the phone in one tap and opens a
$350 agent budget. The first $297 payment captures, leaving $53.
The second $297 hits the wall and voids — over the $53
that remain. Then one refund tap restores the
full $350. Hard caps, enforced by code — not trust.

## 0:45–1:00 | Beat 3: Legacy-vs-Protected Sync + ChatGPT/Claude Connectors

Next, the wallet looks backward and outward. Years of unwitnessed
payments sit beside protected ones carrying the PayPal fingerprint.
Then ChatGPT and
Claude connect as scoped spenders through that same guarded wallet:
one-time credentials, never standing access.

## 1:00–1:25 | Beat 4: Witnessed Checkout ($297.00 Capture) & "Verify It Yourself" Read-Back

Now a normal checkout. VeriPact reads the listing — 27-inch monitor,
brand new, $279 — and an independent witness snapshots it: exact
quotes, a catalog cross-check, a trusted timestamp. Shipping and
tax are missing, so VeriPact flags $41 of headroom. PayPal resolves
the totals, the final charge lands at $297 —
under the cap — and the order is captured. Then your browser
recomputes the fingerprint and reads it back: exact match.

## 1:25–1:42 | Beats 5–6: Hidden $55 Surcharge Auto-Void & Hidden Prompt-Injection Block

Now the traps. A hidden $55 shipping surcharge pushes the total to
$340.50 — over the cap — so VeriPact voids the hold before a cent is
captured. Then a page with invisible instructions telling the agent to
ignore its rules. VeriPact spots the hidden
attack and blocks the purchase before PayPal is ever called.

## 1:42–2:12 | Beats 7–8: Dispute Desk — Buyer Forgery First, Then Merchant Drift

After purchase, the dispute desk. First, a buyer who edited their
receipt to claim 60-day returns. The signature looks fine, but the
fingerprint fails — forgery caught, seller wins. Next, the reverse:
a merchant who quietly
cut returns from 30 days to 14 and added a 25% fee. The buyer's copy
matches the witness; the seller's does not — drift proven, buyer wins.

## 2:12–2:25 | Beat 8 Finale: 72-Run Regression Chart & Close

Finally, one plain-English question charts the full test suite: 24
storefronts, 72 runs, every verdict correct. Buyer controls guard
the spending, and VeriPact proves the promise.

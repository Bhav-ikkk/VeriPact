Executive Summary & Core Positioning1.1 One-Paragraph PitchBuyer-side guardrails (spend caps, mandates, signed intents) control what an AI shopping agent is allowed to spend. Nobody builds proof of what the merchant actually promised—and a 2026 academic benchmark found that an automated shopping agent could determine a merchant's true all-in price at only 18 of 330 retailers. When a return policy changes after checkout, or a seller contests a legitimate claim, the buyer's own unverified log is the only record that ever existed. VeriPact closes that gap: it captures, independently witnesses, and time-anchors the merchant's displayed terms at the moment of checkout, binds that evidence into the PayPal order record itself, and gives sellers a verifiable way to resolve—or fairly contest—a PayPal dispute.Core Positioning Line: "Buyer guardrails control what an agent is allowed to spend. VeriPact proves what the merchant actually promised."2. The Plain-English Guide (For Non-Technical Readers & Reviewers)2.1 How AI Shopping Works TodayInstead of spending an hour comparing twenty browser tabs to buy a computer monitor, consumers and businesses are starting to use AI shopping assistants. A buyer gives their assistant a plain-English instruction:"Buy me a brand-new 27-inch 4K monitor for under $320 total, and make sure the store offers free 30-day returns."The AI searches online stores, checks the product details, and sets up the payment through PayPal.2.2 The Blind Spot Everybody Else MissedWhen companies like Google, Mastercard, Visa, and most hackathon teams look at AI shopping, they focus entirely on controlling the buyer's AI assistant. They build digital spending limits so your AI cannot accidentally spend $500 when your budget was $320. That is useful, but it only solves half of the transaction.Nobody is solving the other half: what happens when the online store changes its story after you pay—or when a dishonest buyer fakes a receipt?Imagine your AI assistant buys that monitor because the store's webpage clearly says "Free 30-Day Returns — $0 Restocking Fee." Ten days later, the monitor arrives with a defect, or you decide to return it. Two symmetrical failures happen today:Failure Mode A — The Shifting Store Page (Merchant Policy Drift):By the time you ask for a refund on Day 12, the store has quietly updated its website to read "14-Day Returns with a 25% Restocking Fee ($74.25 penalty)." When you open a PayPal dispute, the merchant claims the 25% fee was always there. Because a human never looked at the screen during checkout, your only evidence is your AI assistant's own internal log—which any merchant can dismiss as an AI hallucination.Failure Mode B — The Dishonest Buyer (AI-Forged Buyer Receipts):What if we simply let the buyer's AI save a local copy or digital fingerprint of the webpage? A dishonest buyer can easily edit the store's webpage in their own browser before paying—changing "Final Sale" to "60-Day Free Returns plus a $50 Instant Rebate"—and use that faked local record to file a fraudulent PayPal chargeback against an honest merchant.2.3 How VeriPact Solves Both Problems (The Digital Notary Analogy)Think of VeriPact as an independent digital notary standing right next to the PayPal cash register. When an AI assistant is ready to buy an item, VeriPact performs four concrete actions:Reads Only What a Real Human Would See:Before reading a store page, VeriPact strips away invisible trick text that bad actors hide in website code to confuse AI bots. It highlights the exact visible sentences on the screen that state the item's price, condition (New vs. Refurbished), and return policy.Calls an Independent Witness:Instead of trusting the buyer's own computer, a separate Witness Service independently visits the store's public web address from the outside, cross-checks the item against a verified product catalog (Channel3), confirms that the store really is promising "30-Day Free Returns," and seals that record with an official third-party timestamp (DigiCert).Locks the Fingerprint onto the PayPal Order:As PayPal calculates the exact tax and shipping for the buyer's home address, VeriPact stamps a unique 68-character digital fingerprint of those witnessed store terms directly into the official PayPal Order record (custom_id). If the store tries to sneak in a surprise $55 shipping fee at the last second that pushes the price over the buyer's $320 limit, VeriPact automatically cancels (VOIDs) the PayPal hold before a single cent leaves the buyer's account.Settles Contested PayPal Disputes in Seconds:If a dispute happens later, VeriPact's Dispute Verifier Desk (built in AG Studio) compares both sides against the fingerprint stored inside PayPal's own order record:If the merchant changed their policy after the sale, VeriPact proves the buyer's 30-day receipt matches PayPal's order record.If the buyer fabricated a fake receipt, VeriPact catches the forgery and protects the merchant.3. It failed at 178 merchants, of which 137 failed specifically due to undisclosed or address-dependent tax, and 41 failed on shipping.In a separate 555-site access census, roughly one home page in five ($18.9\%$, or 105 sites) could not be read at all by a declared automated client (ranging from $8\%$ on sports/venue sites to $\sim 43\%$ on travel sites). Rule: We never conflate the 555-site access census with the 330-merchant sufficiency sample.Across 4 full end-to-end assessments of large consumer merchants (an airline, a resort, a hardware subscription service, and a hotel brand), zero purchase mandates reached full decision sufficiency pre-checkout, and every determinable criterion rested solely on evidence of class SELF (the agent's unverified self-report). Zero merchant-signed offers were observed prior to checkout.The RAILS Evidence Admissibility Hierarchy (arXiv:2606.08790):Defined by de Valois-Franklin & Bogdan ("RAILS: Verification-Native Clearing for Agentic Commerce"):$$\mathrm{SELF} \preceq \mathrm{SIGN} \preceq \{\mathrm{WIT}, \mathrm{REC}\} \preceq \mathrm{ATT} \preceq \mathrm{PROOF}$$where SELF is a party's own unverified log, SIGN is a cryptographically signed statement by a counter-party, WIT is an independent witness capture of displayed terms, and REC is the payment processor's authoritative transaction record (amount, payee, timestamp, status).Why Signed Mandates Alone Do Not Protect the Decision (arXiv:2609.11757):In "Signing the Transaction but Not the Decision: Whisper Attacks and a Binding Defense for AP2" (Louck, Dvir & Stulman, 2026), the authors demonstrate that current mandate protocols sign the payment authorization but not the merchant offer or context that induced it. 7.2 Visible-Quote Extraction & Substring Verification Schema (ObservedTermSheet)Step 1 (Pre-LLM Deterministic Hidden-Node Scanner):Walks the parsed DOM tree (cheerio / jsdom). Any element matching the following CSS/attribute heuristics is separated into hidden_nodes[]:display:\s*none or visibility:\s*hiddenopacity parsed as float $< 0.05$font-size parsed as $< 2\text{px}$ or $0$position:\s*(absolute|fixed) with left < -500px or top < -500pxaria-hidden="true" or foreground/background luminance contrast ratio $< 1.15$If any string in hidden_nodes[] matches the regex /(ignore\s+previous|system\s+prompt|send\s+to|paypal\.me\/|merchant_id\s*=|override\s+price)/i, the scanner immediately halts and returns gate_decision: "BLOCK_ADVERSARIAL_DOM" without invoking the LLM.Step 2 (Visible Block Indexing):All remaining visible leaf text nodes are indexed as visible_blocks: Array<{ block_id: string, selector: string, text: string }>.Step 3 (LLM Structured Quote Extraction + Deterministic Substring Check):The LLM is given only visible_blocks (with zero payment tools in its tool array) and must return a block_id and verbatim_quote for each attribute. Honesty & Limitations (Stated Up Front in README & Video)A Timestamp Proves Existence, Not Truth: An RFC 3161 token proves a hash existed by time $T$; it is the out-of-band WIT fetch (witness-service) that attests the webpage content was independently observed.Witness Service Operation vs. Production Store Sync: In this hackathon build, witness-service is isolated by process and Ed25519 key from buyer-agent and merchant-checkout to eliminate circular trust, though all three run in our repository. For production deployment, we pitch PayPal Store Sync as the native neutral witness since PayPal already ingests merchant catalog feeds directly.Production vs. Sandbox Adjudication: accept-claim and provide-evidence are real PayPal production dispute actions; adjudicate is a PayPal Sandbox simulation endpoint used to show full dispute closure on camera.Exact Academic Statistics: We always quote arXiv:2610.17378 accurately ("an automated agent could determine an all-in total at only 18 of 330 merchants — tax being the commonest gap") and never conflate it with the separate 555-site access census.Controlled Regression Suite: Because our team authored both the 24 synthetic scenarios and their expected labels, we report results as a controlled regression suite with 95% Wilson confidence intervals, not an independent field benchmark.12.
